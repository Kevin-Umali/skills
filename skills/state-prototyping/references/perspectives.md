# Perspectives

For a stuck decision or a final review. Name the perspective behind a notable choice; a point that fits all three equally is too vague.

- **User.** Is the next step obvious? Does the screen say what is happening, in plain words? Is typed input kept on failure? Does anything jump between states? Keyboard, screen reader, small screen, reduced motion?
- **Developer.** What is fake, and how small is the swap to real? Does it use the existing tokens and components? Which states need a product or backend decision?
- **Agent.** Is every control findable by role and name? Is each outcome readable as text? Does pending say wait and an error say how to recover? Are states reproducible from the URL?
